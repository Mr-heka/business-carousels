# Examples

Fifteen images showing different possibilities. Open the image itself before drawing conclusions; this page only says what to look for. None of them sets a palette, font, person, headline or layout for your business. Prompts are in [example prompts](example-prompts.md); metadata and original hashes are in [index.json](../assets/examples/index.json).

Examples 01 to 15 are fictional demonstrations made for teaching. The businesses, people and processes are invented; they are not staff, customers, testimonials or results.

## Trades and products: visuals that explain

| Image | What it teaches | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [01 Plumbing](../assets/examples/01-plumbing.jpg) | A cutaway shows the cause (buildup, roots) instead of a generic tool icon. Two labels, each on its object. | Kicker is very small at phone size. |
| [02 Roofing](../assets/examples/02-roofing.jpg) | A traced dashed path connects the entry point to the ceiling stain: the arrow carries the whole idea. | Strong example of meaningful arrows. |
| [03 Skincare](../assets/examples/03-skincare.jpg) | Quiet editorial: space, serif headline, soft light. Shows that minimal can still be detailed. | Labels repeat text already printed on the products; labelling the order of use would teach more. Labels are tiny on a phone. |
| [04 Bakery](../assets/examples/04-bakery.jpg) | Playful collage with a three-step sequence and one highlighted figure ("3 DAYS"). | The three-day process is fictional; only claim what is true for your bakery. |
| [05 Accountant](../assets/examples/05-accountant.jpg) | Objects stand in for concepts: profit on the invoice, cash in the wallet, the unpaid invoice between them. | Kicker is small at phone size. |

## People: human-led, Australian settings

| Image | What it teaches | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [11 Electrician](../assets/examples/11-electrician.jpg) | Candid documentary portrait on a Queenslander verandah; the label points to the switchboard, not the person. | The "SWITCHBOARD" label is too small to read on a phone; enlarge labels that carry meaning. |
| [12 Physio](../assets/examples/12-physio.jpg) | A real interaction plus a short step strip (listen, assess, plan) that explains the first visit. | Background wall text is generated set dressing. |
| [13 Childcare](../assets/examples/13-childcare.jpg) | Educator and child, child shown from behind; an informal hand-drawn pointer. | Background signs are generated lettering; check spelling on every sign. |
| [14 Hair salon](../assets/examples/14-hair-salon.jpg) | Honest skin and curl detail, mirror reflection consistent with the scene, serif editorial headline. | Several generated slogans in the background; remove them if they distract. |
| [15 Café](../assets/examples/15-cafe.jpg) | Owner and regular: rapport carries the story, not a giant product shot. | Recurring generated phrase on the chalkboard; generators repeat slogans across images. |

Checked at full size: hands, fingers, eyes, teeth, contact points and reflections. No blocking defect was found in these files; repeat the check on every new image.

## Editorial: one scene, three type treatments

The same generated scene with typography rendered in code from real font files (Anton for headlines, Gloria Hallelujah for handwriting). Figures come from a dated Reuters report (28 September 2026, USD, 2025 financial year) and belong to that source only.

| Image | Treatment | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [21 Restrained bold](../assets/examples/21-editorial-restrained-bold-final.jpg) | Final | Modest heavy headline, generous space, handwritten labels with arrows to the objects, the key qualification ("Reported target; not a completed IPO") kept on the cover. The sculpture heights are not to scale. |
| [22 Strong editorial](../assets/examples/22-editorial-strong-alternative.jpg) | Alternative | Bigger headline and stacked numbers: more punch, less connection between figures and objects. |
| [23 Light editorial](../assets/examples/23-editorial-light-trial.jpg) | Trial | Light headline for a calmer feel. Handwriting stays the real font. |

Keeping final, alternative and trial clearly labelled is part of the lesson.
